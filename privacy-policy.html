Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Carom Billiards 3D: Arena • Last updated: October 1, 2026
Key Summary: We do not collect, track, or share any personal information. Your gameplay data stays on your device, and multiplayer functions rely entirely on Apple's secure infrastructure.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Carom Billiards 3D: Arena ("we", "our", or "the App") respects and protects your personal privacy. We strongly believe in privacy by design: our application is built from the ground up to let you enjoy authentic 3D carom billiards without collecting your personal identity or tracking your behavior.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not track your geographic location (GPS or network location).
  • We do not collect device advertising identifiers (such as IDFA) or browser fingerprints.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs.

3. Local Device Storage

The App saves your gameplay settings, shot history, practice drill progress, and career statistics (such as high run, win rate, and matches played) strictly locally on your device using Apple's standard local storage (UserDefaults). This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the App.

4. Apple Game Center & Multiplayer

The App offers optional online multiplayer matchmaking and leaderboards powered by Apple Game Center (GameKit):

  • Game Center services are governed directly by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Authentication is handled natively by iOS/iPadOS without transmitting login credentials or passwords to us.
  • We do not operate external game servers to store your Game Center profile or friend lists.

5. Local Bluetooth Peer-to-Peer Matches

The App supports direct Bluetooth/Wi-Fi peer-to-peer matches with nearby devices via Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework. This connection operates strictly between the two participating devices without passing through any intermediate cloud servers or recording personal data.

6. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

Carom Billiards 3D: Arena contains zero third-party advertisements and does not integrate ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Facebook Audience Network). No user profile is constructed or monetized for advertising purposes.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our App is suitable for all ages, rated 4+ on the Apple App Store. We do not knowingly solicit or collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Union). Because no personal information is collected at all, parents can feel completely confident about their children's safety while playing.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect product enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this webpage with an updated revision date.
