Game Features

Classic Carom Made Simple

Smooth controls, intuitive ball spin, and exciting game modes designed for everyone to enjoy.

Game Mode
🔴

Three-Cushion (3-Cushion)

The most popular carom challenge! Score points by bouncing the ball off at least 3 cushions before hitting the final ball.

Game Mode
🟡

One-Cushion (1-Cushion)

Fast and fun tactical play. Strike at least one cushion to complete your shot. Great for quick games with friends.

Game Mode
⚪️

Free-Style Carom (Libre)

The classic billiard format. Score continuous points by hitting both object balls freely. Perfect for high scoring runs.

Controls
🌀

Simple Ball Spin & Power

Just tap on the cue ball to add top spin, backspin (draw), or side English. Drag the slider to set shot power effortlessly.

Camera Views
🎥

2D Top-Down & Immersive 3D

Switch seamlessly between an overview top-down angle and a realistic 3D behind-the-cue view with smooth camera zoom.

Solo & Practice
🤖

Smart AI & Fun World Tour

Play solo anytime against Easy, Medium, or Hard AI opponents, or challenge top champions in the World Legends Tour.
