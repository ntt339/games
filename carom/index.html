Tournament Disciplines

3 Classic Carom Modes

Master the world's most prestigious pocketless billiard disciplines with genuine tournament rule sets.

Discipline 1
🔴

Three-Cushion (3-Cushion)

The ultimate strategic test of bank angles and diamond systems. Score points by bouncing the cue ball off at least 3 cushions before hitting the final ball.

Discipline 2
🟡

One-Cushion (1-Cushion)

Fast, dynamic, and tactically rich. Strike at least one cushion prior to completing the carom. Ideal for fine speed control and bank shot precision.

Discipline 3
⚪️

Straight Rail (Libre)

The purest classic billiard format. Score continuous caroms by striking both object balls freely. Perfect for building massive high runs and master gathers.
