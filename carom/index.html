Professional Cue Sports

Championship Carom Disciplines

Meticulously engineered to replicate the real-world physics, strategy, and elegance of official UMB and CEB carom tournaments.

Discipline
🔴

Three-Cushion (3-Băng)

The crown jewel of carom cue sports. Score by striking both object balls while contacting at least 3 cushions beforehand or in-between. Full diamond rail calculation support.

Discipline
🟡

One-Cushion (1-Băng)

Tactical precision requiring at least one cushion contact before completing the carom. Ideal for practicing bank shots, kiss shots, and cushion angles.

Discipline
⚪️

Straight Rail / Libre

The classic carom foundation. Score consecutive caroms with free ball routing. Master the famous American Serie (bida gom) and gather balls along the rail.

Physics Engine
🌀

Simonis Cloth & Spin Dynamics

High-precision numerical integration simulates rolling cloth friction (0.0095), responsive cushion restitution (0.87), English sidespin, follow (cule), draw (trô), and massé curve arcs.

Artificial Intelligence
🤖

Adaptive AI Opponents

Play offline against 3 AI tiers: Easy (beginner-friendly aiming for quick fun), Realistic Medium, and Master AI utilizing bank angle tree search.

Training & Drills
🎯

Pro Drill Academy

Practice 8 curated master drills including Around the Table, Cross Table 3-Cushion, Force Follow, Draw Masse, and Diamond System banking with ghost ball vector guidelines.
