Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

🎱 What carom game modes are available?

The game supports all 3 traditional international carom disciplines: 3-Cushion (Ba Băng), 1-Cushion (Một Băng), and Straight Rail / Libre (Bida Phăng Tự Do), plus dedicated Training Drills and Practice Modes.

🌐 How does Multiplayer work?

You can play multiplayer in 3 distinct ways:
1. Apple Game Center: Auto-match with players worldwide or invite Game Center friends.
2. Bluetooth / Local Network: Play head-to-head with nearby friends without needing an internet connection.
3. Pass & Play: Compete on a single iPhone or iPad device with alternating turns.

🎯 How do I control spin and power?

Use the circular Spin/English touch pad on the screen to position your cue tip (adjusting topspin, draw, or sidespin). Use the vertical Power Slider or pull back on the cue stick to select stroke intensity.

🔄 How do I review or slow down shots?

After any stroke, tap the Replay icon to view exact 3D trajectory ribbons showing ball paths and cushion bounces. You can adjust playback speed between 0.75x slow-mo, 1.0x normal, and 1.5x fast-forward.
