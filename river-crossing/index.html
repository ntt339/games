5 Specialized Puzzle Engines

Unlike generic puzzle apps, River IQ is powered by 5 purpose-built sub-engines modeling distinct mathematical and computer science state machines.

🚣

River Crossing

2-shore & 3-shore island ferry mechanics with pilot credentials, boat capacity, and mutual predator-prey rules.

🧪

Decanting Jugs

Exact volume measuring with liquid jugs (8-5-3L & 16-11-6L) with pour, fill, and empty state trees.

⏳

Night Bridge

Dark bridge crossings with speed-differing family members and finite lantern/hourglass fuel constraints.

⚖️

Scale & Cipher

Ternary balance weighing to isolate counterfeit coins plus multi-dial digital cipher locks.

🐸

Board Grid

Frog swap leaps, Tower of Hanoi discs, Knight's Tour, Peg Solitaire, and Klotski sliding blocks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about gameplay mechanics, hints, and device support.

🚣 How do the river crossing rules work?

Each riddle features specific constraints: a maximum boat capacity, designated pilots, and forbidden combinations (e.g., leaving the Wolf alone with the Sheep, or Demons outnumbering Monks). Tap entities on the shore to board, then tap the destination shore to row.

💡 What does the Hint button do?

The Hint button activates our on-device Breadth-First Search (BFS) graph solver. It analyzes your current state and provides the single next optimal move to navigate out of dead ends without giving away the full solution all at once.

📱 Can I play on iPad and Mac?

Yes! River IQ is a Universal iOS application compiled with arm64 native support for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Macs (M1/M2/M3/M4) with responsive UI that scales crisply to any aspect ratio.

📶 Does River IQ require an active internet connection?

No. River IQ is 100% self-contained and completely playable offline — ideal for flights, commutes, and off-grid relaxation.
