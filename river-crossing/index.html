Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about gameplay mechanics, hints, and device support.

🚣 How do the river crossing rules work?

Each riddle features specific constraints: a maximum boat capacity, designated pilots, and forbidden combinations (e.g., leaving the Wolf alone with the Sheep, or Demons outnumbering Monks). Tap entities on the shore to board, then tap the destination shore to row across.

💡 What does the Hint button do?

The Hint button activates our on-device Breadth-First Search (BFS) graph solver. It analyzes your current state and provides the single next optimal move to navigate out of dead ends without giving away the full solution all at once.

🧠 How is the Brain IQ Certificate calculated?

Each puzzle has an optimal number of trips (S-min). Solving in the minimum moves awards an official 3-star Master Brain rating and IQ Certificate. You can replay any stage at any time to optimize your move efficiency.

📱 Can I play on iPad and Mac?

Yes! River IQ is a Universal iOS application compiled with arm64 native support for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Macs (M1/M2/M3/M4) with a responsive UI that scales crisply to any aspect ratio.

🌐 How do I switch languages?

Tap the Globe icon (🌐) on the Main Menu or in the in-game header. River IQ supports 10 languages: English, Vietnamese, Spanish (Castilian & Mexico), French, German, Danish, Korean, Japanese, and Turkish. All translations switch instantly without restarting.

📶 Does River IQ require an active internet connection?

No. River IQ is 100% self-contained and completely playable offline — ideal for flights, commutes, family road trips, and off-grid relaxation.
