## 5. Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
Embark on a timeless journey of pure deductive reasoning with River IQ: River Crossing — the definitive collection of 35 classic and algorithmic logic riddles, handcrafted for puzzle aficionados of all ages!

From the centuries-old folklore of the Farmer, Wolf, Sheep & Cabbage to the strategic dilemmas of Three Monks & Three Demons and the torchlit crossing of the Night Bridge, River IQ challenges you to transport passengers across treacherous waters while navigating strict predator-prey constraints, boat capacities, and pilot credentials.

Each puzzle represents an elegant mathematical state-space problem with a provable optimal move sequence (S-min). Will you find the shortest path, or will your passengers be left in peril?

=================================
KEY GAME FEATURES
=================================

• 35 MASTER PUZZLE STAGES
Progress through 6 distinct chapters, starting from familiar classic foundations and advancing toward grandmaster-level algorithmic challenges that test your spatial foresight and planning.

• 5 SPECIALIZED PUZZLE ENGINES
Unlike ordinary riddle apps, River IQ features 5 dedicated logic engines:
1. River Crossing: 2-shore & 3-shore island ferry mechanics with pilot qualifications.
2. Decanting Jugs: Precise liquid volume measuring (8-5-3L & 16-11-6L puzzles).
3. Night Bridge & Hourglass: Time optimization with finite torch fuel and varying speeds.
4. Scale & Cipher: Counterfeit coin balance weighing and dial combinations.
5. Board Grid: Toads & Frogs swap leaps, Tower of Hanoi, Knight's Tour, and Peg Solitaire.

• ON-DEVICE BFS AI HINT SOLVER
Never stay stuck! Our built-in Breadth-First Search (BFS) graph algorithm dynamically analyzes your current board position and highlights the exact optimal next move without spoiling the full solution.

• RISK-FREE STEP UNDO & RESTART
Enjoy total creative freedom. The complete Command Pattern history stack allows you to rewind moves step-by-step or restart the level instantly to test fresh hypotheses.

• 10 LOCALIZED LANGUAGES
Seamlessly switch between English, Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt), Spanish (Castilian & Latin American), French, German, Danish, Korean, Japanese, and Turkish with a single tap.

• ASMR SOUNDS & RETINA GRAPHICS
Immerse yourself in gentle river ripples, wooden rowing paddle strokes, and ambient nature soundscapes paired with crisp, clean vector artwork tailored for iPhone and iPad displays.

• 100% OFFLINE & PRIVACY-FIRST
Zero third-party advertisements, zero microtransactions, and zero tracking SDKs. River IQ is fully self-contained and completely playable offline — ideal for flights, commutes, and focused family mental exercises.

=================================
THE 6 PROGRESSIVE CHAPTERS
=================================

• Chapter 1: Classic Foundations (Wolf, Sheep & Cabbage; Monks & Demons; Night Bridge)
• Chapter 2: Tribal & Island Ferries (Jealous Couples, Thief & Police, 3-Shore Islands)
• Chapter 3: Night Bridges & Decanting (Speed Optimization & Exact Volume Jugs)
• Chapter 4: Ciphers & Combinatorics (Balance Scales & Dial Combinations)
• Chapter 5: Chess & Grid Relocations (Frog Leaps, Hanoi Discs & Knight Tours)
• Chapter 6: Grandmaster Algorithmic Challenges (Complex Constraints & Multi-State Traps)

Can you conquer all 35 stages with the minimum possible moves and earn your Master Brain IQ Certificate? 

Download River IQ today and put your logical deduction to the ultimate test!
```
